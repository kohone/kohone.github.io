Nardy

Check every roll yourself.

Backgammon and long nardy against a strong computer that explains, with dice sealed before the game.

For anyone who has ever suspected an app of rigging the dice, and for anyone learning to play better.

Coming to the App Store

Questions: support@kohone.net

Sealed before the game. Opened after it.

The app seals the whole sequence of rolls before the first move and shows its fingerprint. After the game it gives you the key, and you recompute every roll yourself. The computer's level never touches the dice, and neither does any purchase.

Check the dice

Open “Check the dice” from the game. You see the fingerprint and can add your own phrase. When the game is over, tap “Verify” to match the fingerprint and every roll — or recompute them with the verify_dice.py script.

What it does

Two games, full rules

Backgammon with the doubling cube, a single game or a match to 3, 5, 7 or 11 points, the Crawford rule. Long nardy under standard tournament rules.

Computer or a friend

Five levels from beginner to master, or two players on one phone. Choose your colour and the side you bear off to.

Hint and coach

The hint shows the best move on the board. After a mistake the coach says why, in plain words — tap “Why?”.

Game review

After the game: your error rate, the luck of both sides and your costliest moves. Play on from any move with the same dice.

Your own dice

Roll real dice and enter the result on the on-screen pad, or replay a finished game with your own dice.

Dice statistics

Doubles, entries from the bar and hits against the range of fair dice, with an experiment of 1,000 rolls or a simulation of 1,000,000.

Lessons

Rules lessons on a live board, five steps each, and pointers in your first three games.

30 boards, your way

Six boards are free. Simple mode, colour-blind highlights, light and dark, English, Russian, Turkish and German.

What it does not do

  • It never changes the dice: not for a level, not for a purchase.
  • No coins, no boosts, nothing that buys a better roll.
  • No ads during a game.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No subscription.
  • No online play against people.

Data and privacy

Your games, history, statistics and settings are stored on your iPhone; we have no servers and receive none of it. If you switch it on, a copy goes to your own iCloud, which we cannot see. The one third party is advertising in the free version (Google AdMob), and iOS asks your permission before any tracking.

Questions

Are the dice really random?

You do not have to take our word for it: every game's rolls come from a sequence sealed before the first move, and after the game the key lets you recompute each one.

Does the computer see my next roll?

No. Future rolls are never shown, and a replay with your own dice makes the computer play the same moves.

Does a higher level or a purchase give better dice?

No. The level changes only the computer's moves, and no purchase touches the dice.

Which rules does long nardy use?

Standard tournament rules: one checker from the head per turn (except 6-6, 4-4 and 3-3 on the first roll), no six-point wall in front of all the opponent's checkers, and mars.

Are there coins or a subscription?

No: Pro is one purchase, there are no coins, and nothing is a subscription.

Do I still see ads after buying Pro?

No. Pro removes ads and the hint limit for good.

Can I play against people online?

No. You play the computer or a friend on the same phone.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. The computer and the hints run on your iPhone; ads and the iCloud copy wait until you are online.
