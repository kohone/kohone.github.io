Backgammon: Fair Dice

Coming to the App Store

Backgammon and long nardy against a strong computer or a friend on the same phone. Dice you can verify, a coach that explains, and it works offline.

Why this one

Dice you can verify

Before each game the rolls are sealed and you see their fingerprint. After the game you get the key and can recompute every roll — in the app, on this site, in Python or CyberChef.

Tested on 100 million rolls

The same generator as in your games, checked with standard statistical tests. Read the report.

A computer that explains

Five levels from beginner to master. After a mistake the coach says what went wrong in plain words.

Two games, full rules

Backgammon with the doubling cube, Crawford and matches up to 11 points; long nardy under standard tournament rules.

Learn as you play

Rules lessons on a live board, game review with error rate, replay any position with the same dice.

Your own dice

Roll real dice and type the result — the app keeps score, the computer plays its moves.

Free and Pro

Free

  • Both games, all five levels, matches and the cube
  • Dice verification, statistics, review, lessons
  • 6 boards
  • 3 hints a day against the computer, +3 for an optional video
  • A short ad after every second game (none in your first games, never during a game)

Pro — one-time purchase

  • No ads
  • Unlimited hints

Bought once in the app through the App Store, yours forever, restored on your other devices.

Boards

  • 24 more boards, each sold separately in the app
  • Preview any board before you buy
