Frequently asked

How do I mark a period? Tap the day your period started in the calendar. Tap a day to mark it, and hold a day for sex, flow, symptoms and a note.

Why is the forecast a range of dates? When your cycles vary, the forecast shows a range. It sharpens after two marked periods. Before that, your usual cycle length is used if you set it.

I forgot to mark some days. Edit any past day, or use “Add past periods” to enter periods by their dates. Days after today cannot be marked.

Reminders don't arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for Aylin in iPhone Settings, then turn the reminder on again.

Health doesn't take my days. Allow Aylin in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices. Days written to Health by another app can be removed only there.

How do I move my records? In Settings, export your records as a table, or import from a file. Import adds only the days that have no record yet, and the first line of the file must read: date,flow,symptoms,note.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Delete all data. Period days already written to Health stay there; delete them in the Health app.

I bought Aylin Plus on another iPhone. Use “Restore purchases” on the purchase screen.
