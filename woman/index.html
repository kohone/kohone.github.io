Aylin

Coming to the App Store

A period calendar. Tap a day to mark it, see the day of your cycle and when the next period is expected. Your records are stored only on this iPhone.

  • Mark with a tap. Tap a day to mark your period. Hold a day for flow, symptoms and a note; double tap marks sex. Flow runs from spotting to heavy. You can edit any past day, and add past periods by dates.
  • Your cycle. The cycle day and the days until your period, with a calendar for the month. The forecast is a range of dates when your cycles vary, and it sharpens after two marked periods. If you know your usual cycle length, it is used until then.
  • Fertile days, if you want them. Estimated fertile days and ovulation in the calendar, from cycle length. An estimate, not a measurement and not birth control.
  • History. Each period that started in the dates you choose: how long it lasted and how long the cycle was, next to your usual length and range.
  • Symptoms and notes. A short list — cramps, headache, back pain, breast tenderness, bloating, fatigue, mood changes, acne, nausea — and a note for any day.
  • Reminders and a widget. A reminder before your period, with your own text. A Home Screen widget shows the days left.
  • Apple Health, both ways. Period days you mark appear in Health, and period days added or removed in Health change here. Optional.
  • Lock. Lock Aylin with Face ID or your iPhone passcode.
  • Your data. Export your records as a table, import from a file, or delete all data from Settings.
  • Languages. English and Ukrainian.

Aylin Plus

One purchase, forever — not a subscription. It unlocks the report for a doctor (a PDF of one or two pages with marked periods, cycle lengths, symptoms and notes), charts, extra reminders, your own symptoms, and themes and icons. Marking days, the forecast, history, reminders and export stay free.
