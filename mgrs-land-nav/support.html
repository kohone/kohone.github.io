Frequently asked

Why do the last digits of my grid change while I stand still? GPS on a phone is accurate to a few metres; the app shows the accuracy next to the grid. For a 10-digit grid (1 m) the last digits will move.

Why truncated, not rounded? An MGRS grid names the south-west corner of its square, the way you read it from a map. Rounding could put you in the next square.

Which north is this azimuth from? Every azimuth is labelled Grid, Mag or True. Change it with the picker above the number.

The compass looks wrong. Move away from metal and cars and wave the phone in a figure 8. The app warns when compass accuracy is poor.

I bought Pro on another iPhone. Settings → Restore purchase.

Can I use it on a graded land nav course? No — phones are not allowed there. Use it to practise before.
