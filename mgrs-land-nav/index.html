MGRS Land Nav – Military GPS

Coming to the App Store

Your position as a big, clear MGRS grid — and a trainer for the land navigation skills you need when the phone goes in the bag.

  • Position. 10- or 8-digit MGRS from GPS, truncated like on a map, never rounded. UTM, lat/lon, and the G-M angle for where you stand.
  • Points and courses. Type a grid the way you read it from a map, project a point by azimuth and distance, plan legs in mils with pace counts. GPX in and out.
  • Train. Calibrate your pace count, walk a blind leg and see exactly where you drifted, drill 8-digit plotting.
  • Honest note. A training aid: on a graded land navigation course your phone is not allowed.

No ads, no account, no tracking.
