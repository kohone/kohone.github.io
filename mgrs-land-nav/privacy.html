Privacy Policy — MGRS Land Nav – Military GPS

Last updated: 2026-09-26

MGRS Land Nav collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of ours.

What the app uses, and where it stays

  • Location (only while the app is open): to show your grid, azimuths and distances, and to fix the start and finish of a training leg. It is processed on your iPhone and never sent to us.
  • Motion (pedometer step counts): to count your steps between the start and finish of a pace-count walk or a training leg. Read on your iPhone only.
  • Your points, courses and training history: stored in the app's storage on your iPhone. There is no cloud copy.
  • Settings (units, north reference, grid digits): stored on your iPhone.

The map

The map screen shows Apple Maps. To draw it, your iPhone requests map tiles for the area on screen from Apple, as every app with Apple Maps does. Apple handles those requests under its own privacy policy; your points and training data are not sent with them. Everything else works offline.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; we do not receive your payment details or personal information.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • Your points, courses and training records stay on your iPhone until you delete them in the app (swipe to delete) or delete the app, which removes all of them.
  • We keep nothing, because we receive nothing.

Your choices

  • Allow or stop location access: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → MGRS Land Nav.
  • Allow or stop motion access: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: support@kohone.net
