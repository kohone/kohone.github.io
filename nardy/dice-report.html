Fair dice: 100 million rolls

We ran the app's real dice generator: 1,000,000 new games of 100 rolls, each with a fresh key from the system's cryptographic generator — exactly as in a game.

Results

  • Doubles: 16.6620% (expected 16.6667%; deviation −1.26 σ — ordinary chance).
  • Pips per roll: 8.1669 (expected 8.1667).
  • Longest run of doubles in a row: 9. Runs of 3, 4 and 5 doubles: 315,515, 51,911 and 8,610 — each longer run about 6 times rarer, as it should be.
