Privacy Policy — Bowling Score Keeper

Last updated: 2026-09-25

The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising. There is no server of ours for your data to go to.

What stays on your device

Your sessions, games, rolls, marked pins, balls and their photos are stored on your iPhone in the app's own storage. Photos you pick for a ball are copied into the app's folder and never leave it.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud and the app is allowed to use it, your data is also kept in your own private iCloud database so a new phone does not start empty. That data belongs to your Apple Account: we have no access to it and cannot read it. Turning iCloud off for the app keeps everything local.

Files you create yourself

Export produces a CSV or PDF file. Where it goes — Files, mail, another app — is entirely your choice; the app only hands the file to the system share sheet.

What we do not collect

  • No identifiers for advertising, no tracking across apps or sites.
  • No usage analytics, no crash SDKs from third parties.
  • No contacts, no location, no health data, no microphone.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your card, and we do not receive your name or address.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: support@kohone.net
