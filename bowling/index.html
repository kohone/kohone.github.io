Bowling Score Keeper

Coming to the App Store

A scorekeeper for ten-pin bowling: enter a game frame by frame, mark the pins you leave standing, and see where your points actually go.

What it does

  • Frame-by-frame entry with the tenth frame handled by the USBC rules, or a single final score when you are in a hurry.
  • As many games in a session as you played — four, five, a whole tournament.
  • Any roll, game or session can be corrected or deleted afterwards.
  • Average by month, strike rate, spares split into single pins, multi-pin leaves and splits.
  • A table of every leave: which ones you convert and which you do not.
  • Your arsenal of balls, with the average for each one.
  • Export to CSV and PDF, import from CSV.

What it does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads and no trackers.
  • It does not read the scoreboard with the camera and does not track the ball down the lane.

Entering games and seeing your whole history are free. The breakdown — average by month, the leave table, the percentages, the filters and export — is the paid part.
