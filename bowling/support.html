Support — Bowling Score Keeper

Written by one person. Write to support@kohone.net and you will get an answer from that person.

Before you write

  • A score looks wrong. Tell me the frames as you bowled them — the counting is covered by tests against the USBC rules, so a real mistake is worth finding.
  • Moving to a new phone. Turn iCloud on for the app on both phones, or export to CSV on the old one and import it on the new one.
  • A session ruined your average. Any session, game or roll can be deleted or corrected.
  • Restoring a purchase. The Data tab has the restore action; it is free and works on any device with the same Apple Account.

What helps me answer faster

The iOS version, the app version, and what you expected to see instead. A screenshot is better than a description.
