Frequently asked

Why does a day say “No moonrise today”? The moon rises about 50 minutes later each day, so roughly once a month it rises after midnight and skips a calendar day. The app says so instead of showing a time from another day.

Why are the times different from another app or site? Check the time zone of the place (Places tab). We compute times in the time zone of the place, not your phone, and round to the nearest minute like the U.S. Naval Observatory.

How is the day score made? Tap “Why this score?” on the Today screen — the formula and the reasons for that day are shown.

12- or 24-hour time? The app follows your iPhone: Settings → General → Date & Time.

Alerts didn't arrive. Alerts are planned two weeks ahead for the selected place. Open the app now and then, and check that notifications are allowed for Solunar Times.

I bought Pro on another iPhone. Settings → Restore Purchases.
