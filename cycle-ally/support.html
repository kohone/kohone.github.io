Support — Cycle Ally

Write to us: support@kohone.net. One person reads that mailbox. Please do not send entries from your log; a screenshot of the problem and your iOS version help.

Frequently asked

How do I log a period? On Today, tap Started, Spotting or Ended. Undo is in the confirmation line. Past periods can be added with “Past period” or edited in Calendar.

Why is a cycle left out of my numbers? You may have marked it Not typical in Calendar. It stays in history but is not counted.

Reminders don't arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for Cycle Ally in iPhone Settings, then check the reminder on the medication.

Apple Health doesn't take my entries. Allow Cycle Ally in the Health app under Data Access & Devices, then switch saving on again in Settings.

How do I move to a new iPhone? Make a backup file in Settings, choose a password you will remember, and restore it from the Welcome screen on the new phone. Without the password the file cannot be opened.

How do I get a spreadsheet? Export spreadsheet (CSV) in Settings.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Delete all data. It is permanent; make a backup file first if you may want the log back. Entries already saved to Apple Health stay there; remove them in the Health app.

I bought Plus on another iPhone. Use Restore purchases in Settings, in Cycle Ally Plus.
