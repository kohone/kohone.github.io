Cycle Ally

Your patterns. A clearer picture.

A calm PCOS and PMOS journal that helps you bring your history to your next appointment.

For anyone with PCOS or PMOS, or heading to a first visit to find out, whatever the length of your cycles.

Coming to the App Store

Questions: support@kohone.net

Bring facts to your doctor.

Cycle Ally keeps what you log and counts it, and nothing else. It never calls your body right or wrong: any comparison is only with your own history, and a day you did not log is not counted as a day with nothing.

Log what happens, in your time

Tap a tile on Today, or open the day for the whole picture. The numbers come from what you wrote, with the count of logged days beside them.

What it does

Today

The day of your cycle, the lengths of your own cycles, and one-touch Started, Spotting and Ended. A cycle of any length counts, with no upper limit.

A day, in one scroll

Flow, symptoms from None to Severe, mood, sleep, tags and a note — or Nothing today. You can add trackers of your own: a scale, yes/no, a number or a choice.

Medications

Doses and the times you take them, dose changes, side effects and injection sites. Mark Taken or Skip from the lock screen.

Calendar

Month, Year and Cycles. Edit periods by range, and mark a cycle Not typical so it stays in history but out of the counts.

Trends

Cycle lengths and how often each symptom turned up, always shown against the days you logged, such as 14 of 63.

Before and after

What you logged around a medication start or a dose change, side by side, with the other changes of the same weeks listed next to it.

Care

Visits, questions for your doctor, what changed since the last visit, lab results, check-ups and a timeline of periods, medications and visits.

Summary for your doctor

A PDF of your own entries for the span and sections you choose, with a preview before you send it. Your data, backup and lock are in Settings.

What it does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no code from other companies.
  • No server of ours that holds your log, and no iCloud sync of its own.
  • No diagnosis and no medical advice.
  • No “late” or “irregular” verdicts on your cycle.
  • Not for contraception, and no fertile-window predictions.
  • No weight goals, calorie counts or diets.

Data and privacy

Everything you log is stored on your iPhone, and we do not receive it. The app contains no analytics and no code from other companies, and it makes no network requests of its own; only the purchase goes through Apple.

Apple Health is used only if you allow it, and Face ID or your passcode can lock the app. You can export a spreadsheet, make an encrypted backup file, or erase everything with “Delete all data” in Settings.

Questions

Will it call my cycle late or irregular?

No: the app never calls your cycle late, irregular or abnormal. If your period has not come, Today only asks “Has your period started?” and lets you pick the day.

What if my cycles are very long?

A cycle of 120 or 200 days counts like any other: it appears in your lengths, charts and summary, and there is no upper limit.

Where is my log kept?

Only on your iPhone: there is no account and no server of ours, and the app itself uses no iCloud. If you back up your whole iPhone to iCloud, iOS includes the app's storage like any other app's.

Is it free?

Yes: the whole log is free, and Cycle Ally Plus is an optional subscription for longer spans and extra comparisons.

Can I take it to my doctor?

Yes: the app makes a PDF summary of your entries. Cycles for 12 months and symptoms and medications for 3 months are free; 6 or 12 months with lab results is Plus.

Does it tell me whether I have PCOS or what to take?

No: it does not diagnose or give medical advice, and it is not for contraception. It organises what you wrote, and compares it only with your own history.

Can I bring my records or move to a new iPhone?

Yes: you can import history from Apple Health or from another app's export file, and move everything with an encrypted backup file locked by a password you choose.

Will a reminder give anything away on my lock screen?

Reminders are kept neutral: the title is just “Cycle Ally”, and each medication has its own switch for showing its name.
