Privacy Policy — Cycle Ally

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Cycle Ally has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no code from other companies. What you log stays on your iPhone, and we do not receive it.

What the app stores, and where

Periods and their details, symptoms and your own trackers, mood, sleep, notes, tags, medications with dose changes and side effects, visits, questions for your doctor, lab results, check-up dates, home test readings, photos you add to the diary and your settings are stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. They are not sent to us and the app makes no network requests of its own. The widget reads a snapshot kept in storage shared only between the app and its widget.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications planned on your iPhone. No push server is involved. The title is “Cycle Ally”, and each medication has its own switch for showing its name.

Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, Cycle Ally reads menstrual flow, spotting, symptoms and the medications you choose from Apple Health to fill in your history, and saves the period days, spotting and symptoms you enter back to Health. Dates you marked as approximate are never written. This stays within your iPhone's Health data; you can change the permission in the Health app. Health data is used only for the app's own features. We do not receive it, and it is never used for advertising or sold. “Delete all data” does not touch Apple Health; remove what is there in the Health app.

Face ID, camera and photos

The lock uses the system's authentication; the app never receives your face data or passcode. The camera is used only if you take a photo of a lab sheet or a visit note, and the photo stays in Cycle Ally on your iPhone, locked while the phone is locked. Photos you pick from your library are chosen through the system picker.

Files you make and send

The PDF summary for your doctor, the spreadsheet (CSV) and the encrypted backup file are created on your iPhone only when you ask. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to share or save the file, and then it goes where you send it. The backup is locked with a password you choose; we never see it and cannot recover it.

iPhone backups

The app uses no iCloud itself. If you back up your whole iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, iOS includes the app's storage in that backup like any other app's, under Apple's terms. Settings → Privacy lets you leave Cycle Ally out of the iPhone backup; then only your own backup file can restore the log on a new phone.

Purchases

Cycle Ally Plus is sold by Apple. Payment and billing details are handled by Apple, and the app is told only whether a subscription is active. We do not receive your name, card or Apple Account.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • Your log stays on your iPhone until you delete it: “Delete all data” in Settings erases the database, attachments and settings at once and cannot be undone. Deleting the app also removes it.
  • Files you exported stay wherever you put them.
  • If you write to support, we keep the correspondence only as long as needed to help you. Please do not send entries from your log.

Consumer health data

We do not collect your consumer health data: it stays on your iPhone, and we hold no copy, share it with nobody and sell it to nobody. To ask about this, write to support@kohone.net.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we collect nothing from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: support@kohone.net

Cycle Ally is a personal log. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
